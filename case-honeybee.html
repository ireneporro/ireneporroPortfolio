≡Honeybee HealthWellness / Subscription website

Web design · UI redesign · Prototype

Many wellness apps,
one membership.

Honeybee bundles virtual care, therapy, meditation, fitness and more into one monthly plan. I first designed the site in 2023. This is a redesign of that work: the same product and flows, with a clearer structure, one visual system and a working prototype of the whole journey, from the landing page to cancelling.

Concept redesign of a 2023 project. Partner apps are replaced with invented services, people photos are free stock images, and all data is illustrative.

01 / What changed

Same idea, cleaner execution.

The concept worked: show what is inside the plan, make the plans easy to compare and keep sign-up short. The execution did not hold up. Teal, yellow and green competed for attention, text was small, hexagons filled every empty space and the partner grid left an orphan card. What I kept on purpose: real people, app icons around the phone and the light blue of the original brand.

  • One paletteHoney yellow for highlights, deep teal for every action, light blue from the original brand for backgrounds and icons, cream as the base.
  • One type familyFigtree for everything, with a clear scale: 64px headlines, 19px lead text, nothing under 13px.
  • Invented servicesReal partner logos are replaced with eight named services that share one icon style, in a grid of 4 × 2.
  • People firstEvery service, review and key moment shows a person, so the site feels like wellness and not like a pricing table.

02 / Landing

Showing what is inside the plan.

The hero answers the main question in one sentence and shows the product in use: a phone with today’s therapy session, a meditation and a class, with the eight services slowly orbiting around it. Below, each service is a card with a photo, what it covers and which plan includes it. Tapping a card opens its details.

The page then explains the three steps to get started, shows member reviews and answers the questions people ask before paying. It ends with the app: one place for every service.

03 / Plans

Three plans, monthly or yearly.

Foundation, Enhanced and Elite share one layout, so the only thing that changes is what is included. Enhanced is marked as the most popular. The yearly toggle shows the monthly price and the total saving under each plan, and a comparison table below lists every service.

04 / Checkout

Two short steps, with the plan always visible.

Sign-up and payment sit next to the same summary: the plan, what it includes, the free trial and what is due today. Members can switch plan or billing period from the summary without going back. Errors show under each field, and the confirmation page says what happens next.

05 / Account and cancellation

Getting set up, and leaving fairly.

After paying, members land on a setup page where each service is activated with one tap, with progress at the top. The plan card shows the trial end date and the next charge.

Cancelling takes three steps: a reason, an offer of 50% off the next month, then a clear confirmation that services stay active until the paid period ends. Keeping the plan or cancelling are both one click.

Interactive prototype

From the landing page to cancelling.

Pick a plan, switch between monthly and yearly, create an account and pay with the test card. Then activate your services, book a doctor visit, upgrade, or try to cancel and see the retention offer.
