🐝Honeybee HealthWellness / Subscription website

UI design · Responsive web

A website for a monthly
wellness subscription.

Honeybee bundles several wellness apps (virtual care, therapy, meditation, fitness) into one monthly plan. The job was mostly visual: redesign the site so the plans and what is included are easy to understand, and give the brand a lighter, friendlier look.

01 / Landing

Showing what is inside the plan.

The old site listed partner apps as a block of text. I turned each one into a card with its icon, a short line about what it does and a link, and placed the most popular apps around the hero image so visitors see what they get before scrolling.

The hexagon pattern and the honey yellow come from the name. Teal is used for actions.

02 / Plans

Three plans, monthly or yearly.

Foundation, Enhanced and Elite share one layout, so the only thing that changes is what is included. The yearly toggle shows the savings right under each price.

03 / Sign-up and account

From choosing a plan to the confirmation page.

Sign-up, payment and the confirmation page follow the same structure, with the selected plan always visible. I also designed the cancellation page, which offers a discount before the member leaves.
