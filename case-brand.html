Two smaller projects that show the visual side of my work: a style guide, menu and social posts for a taqueria, and a brand manual for the product studio I worked at.

01 / El Burro · Tacos & Beer

A style guide, a menu and a social feed for a taqueria.

El Burro needed a look that worked on Instagram and in the restaurant. I built a small style guide (a red and yellow palette, Baloo for headlines and a hand-drawn accent font), then applied it to the printed menu and a set of social posts that mix food photography with bold type.

02 / Product studio brand manual

Brand guidelines for a no-code product studio.

For the agency I worked at, I wrote and designed the brand manual: logo and its variants, the slash symbol, colors, typography, logo rules and how to use the brand on the website, social media and presentations. The studio name is changed here; colors, type and rules are the real ones.
